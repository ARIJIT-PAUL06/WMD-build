The Problem: The Delhi Inversion Trap

During post-monsoon and winter months, the boundary-layer in Delhi-NCR collapses to < 300m, trapping PM2.5, NO₂, and biomass smoke at human breathing levels.

Existing government apps report retrospective hourly averages after exposure occurs. School headmasters receive no forward warning before 7:00 AM school bus arrival, leading to hazardous outdoor morning assemblies and severe acute respiratory distress.

The V2 Breakthrough: True Autonomy

The Three Pillars of Autonomous Intervention

Target: School Principals, Headmasters, Clinic Directors, Hospital Administrators.

PILLAR 2: RAPID 5KM × 5KM BLOCK EMERGENCY SURGE ALERTS
